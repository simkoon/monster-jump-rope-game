| skip → guarded play actions | the skipping tap must not reach the next turn's buttons |

| T-08-32 | Denial of Service (listener leak) | useTapToSkip | low | mitigate | listeners exist only while a skippable beat is current and are removed on disable / unmount; spy-balance unit test (D-23) |
